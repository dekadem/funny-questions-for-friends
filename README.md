# 30 Funny Questions to Ask Your Friends (That Actually Get Answers)

Need conversation starters that break the ice without going awkward? Here are 30 funny questions that work for friend hangouts, road trips, and game nights — plus a free iPhone & iPad deck if you want to play them face-to-face.

## The questions

1. What's the weirdest food combination you secretly love?
2. Which childhood cartoon character would be the worst roommate?
3. What's a hill you're willing to die on that nobody else agrees with?
4. If your life had a theme song for the next week, what would it be?
5. What's the most ridiculous thing you've Googled this month?
6. Which friend would survive a zombie apocalypse — and who would get eaten first?
7. What's a skill you have that is completely useless but you are weirdly proud of?
8. If you had to eat only one cuisine for a year, what would it be?
9. What's the pettiest reason you've ever ended an argument?
10. Which emoji do you overuse and refuse to stop?
11. What's a movie you pretend to like so people stop recommending better ones?
12. If you could rename yourself for a week, what name would you pick?
13. What's the worst fashion trend you participated in?
14. Which household chore would you outsource forever if you could?
15. What's a conspiracy theory you half-believe for fun?
16. If your friends were a boy band, what would your group name be?
17. What's the most unhinged autocorrect fail you've sent?
18. Which celebrity would be chaos as your coworker?
19. What's a snack you gatekeep?
20. If you had to teach a one-hour class on anything, what would it be?
21. What's the funniest misunderstanding you've had with a stranger?
22. Which app do you open when you are avoiding real work?
23. What's a compliment you received that was weirdly specific?
24. If your week were a weather report, what would it say?
25. What's the silliest fear you still have?
26. Which friend always has the wildest stories — prove it with one?
27. What's a TV show you rewatch when your brain needs soft noise?
28. If you could ban one small talk question forever, which one?
29. What's the most "main character" thing you've done this year?
30. Who in this group would win a silent staring contest?
